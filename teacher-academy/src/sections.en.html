Framework

Where this programme sits

The Cambridge English Teaching Framework describes a teacher's development in four stages. The Teaching Compass targets the first of them, Foundation: the shared pedagogical ground everyone who teaches with us builds on.

  1. FoundationThis programme
  2. Developing
  3. Proficient
  4. Expert

The other three stages are outside the scope of this programme.

Roadmap

From module to certificate, in four stops

Each module is a short reading that ends with a five-question check. Four correct answers open the next module; after all ten you can take the certification exam.

  1. 1

    Ten modules

    They open in order, and you go at your own pace.

  2. 2

    Module checks

    5 questions at the end of each module; at least 4 correct.

  3. 3

    Certification exam

    30 scenario questions, pass mark 80%.

  4. 4

    Certificate and badge

    A record of completion with a publicly verifiable code.

  5. →

    Then: interview and agreement

    The last step of joining the team. See the process

Time

How much time should you set aside?

Each module takes an estimated 14 to 18 minutes to read. With the checks and at your own pace, the whole programme takes about three hours; there is no rush, and your progress is stored in your browser.

The academy is the third step of joining our team. Not required to apply, but recommended: it is the best description of how we teach.

  1. 1

    Apply

    Fill in the form honestly and attach your CV; the two motivation questions matter most.

  2. 2

    We review and invite

    We look at qualifications, experience and fit; if there is a fit, we invite you to your teacher account.

  3. 3You are here

    Educator Academy

    Complete our free ten-module course for teachers and earn your certificate.

  4. 4

    Interview and agreement

    A 30-minute online interview, then your agreement. Once you are approved, students can book lessons with you.
